# Example City Library

## Job Posting

The Example City Library serves 300,000 residents across twelve branches. Our
website is the front door for most of them: opening hours, events, the
catalogue and our local-history collection.

As Digital Editor you own the website's content. You write and edit pages,
keep the information on twelve branches current, and work with our local-history
team to publish collection stories. You manage the CMS together with an external
developer and set the editorial guidelines for colleagues who contribute.

You bring:
- Experience as a web or digital editor
- Writing in plain language for a broad audience
- Experience with a CMS (we use Drupal)
- Affinity with libraries, archives or heritage

32 hours a week. Salary €3,600 - €4,900 gross a month.
