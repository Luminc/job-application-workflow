## Who I am

Robin is a digital editor and content designer with a background in archives and
publishing. From 2019 to 2024 Robin worked at the Harbourline Archive, a regional
archive, as digital editor: writing and editing online collection stories,
running the collection website (Drupal), and setting up the image workflow for
digitised maps and photographs. Before that, two years as production editor at
Northfield Press, a small non-fiction publisher.

Since 2024 Robin has worked freelance for cultural and public organisations:
content audits, rewriting service pages to plain language, and migrating
websites to a new CMS.

**The thread:** making collections and complex information findable and
readable for people who are not experts. Robin is most motivated when the
content matters (heritage, public information, education) and least when the
work is campaign volume for its own sake.

**Strengths**
- Turning expert material into plain, accurate text without losing substance
- Structure: information architecture, metadata, content models in a CMS
- Owning a workflow end to end: research, writing, images, publication
- Calm in a migration: inventories, redirects, testing

## Evidence

### Harbourline Archive (2019–2024, always past tense)
- Wrote and edited ~120 collection stories for the website; editor of the monthly newsletter (6,000 subscribers)
- Ran the collection website in Drupal; designed the content model for "stories" linked to archive records
- Set up the image workflow for digitised maps and photographs: file naming, rights metadata, export sizes
- Led the move of the visitor information pages to plain language (B1), tested with visitors

### Northfield Press (2017–2019)
- Production editor for non-fiction titles: copy-editing, proofs, schedules with designers and printers

### Freelance (2024–now)
- Content audit and plain-language rewrite of service pages for a municipal library network
- Website migration for a small foundation (WordPress to a headless CMS): inventory, redirects, QA

### Education
- MA Cultural Heritage (2017), BA History (2015)

## What this search is for

**Hours and pay**
- Preferred 28–32 hours a week; full-time is fine when the role is a clear step up.
- Pay floor around €3,400 gross a month for 32 hours; below that, name the gap rather than reject.
- Freelance: day rate from €400; a lower rate is not an automatic no, but say so.

**Location:** Utrecht or within ~50 km; hybrid or remote within the Netherlands.

**Good fit**
- Digital editor, content designer, content strategist, web editor
- CMS or website owner with a real content role
- Collections, archives, libraries, publishing, public information, education

**Not a fit**
- Pure marketing or growth roles, social media scheduling
- News reporting
- Junior roles, internships
- Software development

**Match or bridge:** a role does not have to be ideal. A bridge role is fine if it
brings growth, new skills or a strong team, not only income.

## Positioning

- Lead with the side of the experience that fits the role: editorial, structural or migration.
- Present the mix of writing and structure as one way of working, not a list of skills.
- Gaps: name them briefly and honestly, then turn to an adjacent skill. No apologies.

## Lessons learned

- Specific beats generic: name one concrete project of the organisation.
- Plain-language work is the strongest evidence for public organisations; lead with it there.
- Letters written for roles that did not resonate never led to an interview; do not force a mission fit.
